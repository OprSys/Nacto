@MEM_CLEARVM
- Description: Clears the entire VM register array.
- Note: Register 1 is the amount of registers to wipe (being all of them), and register 2 is the starting index (we can't wipe register 1 and 2 for now as they're being used).
SETVAL 1 15
SETVAL 2 3
JEQ x2 x1 +4
SETVAL x2 0
ADD 2 x2 1
JMP -3
SETVAL 1 0
SETVAL 2 0
RET
@\

@MEM_AUTOLEN
- Description: Automatically gets length from LRAM by scanning and stopping at the first 0.
- Param x1: The highslot to scan.
- Return x0: The length.
SETVAL 0 0
PLOAD 2 x1 x0
JZ 2 +3
ADD 0 x0 1
JMP -3
RET
@\
