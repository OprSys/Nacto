INCLUDE /INCL/MEM

@PROC_SPAWN
- Description: Executes a binary or executable, returning a PID for the spawned process.
- Param x1: Highslot for path.
- Return x0: The PID.
SETVAL 1 x1
SETVAL 6 x1
GO MEM_AUTOLEN
SETVAL 2 x6
SETVAL 6 0
SETVAL 1 x0
SETVAL 0 9
SETVAL 3 0
SYSCALL
RET
@\

@PROC_WAITPID
- Description: Takes a PID and blocks the current program until the specified process terminates.
- Param x1: The PID.
SETVAL 0 10
SETVAL 1 x1
SYSCALL
RET
@\
